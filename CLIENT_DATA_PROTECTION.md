# How BuildMind Protects Your Data

_A short overview for clients evaluating the BuildMind PMO platform._

BuildMind is a project-governance platform for major capital projects. We know the
information you put into it — budgets, risks, schedules, commercial data — is
sensitive. Here is how we keep it protected.

### Your data is isolated from every other client
Each client organization is a separate, walled-off tenant. You see only your own
organization's data, and no other client can see yours. This separation is enforced
at the data layer on every record — not just hidden in the interface — and we verify
it by testing access as a second organization before anyone is given access.

### Access is invite-only and role-based
- The platform is private: it requires a sign-in, and only people we invite can get in.
- Each person is granted a role that controls what they can see and do.
- Administrative functions (managing users and organizations) are restricted to
  authorized BuildMind staff only.

### Encryption
Your data is encrypted in transit (secure HTTPS connections) and encrypted at rest
in the database.

### Built on certified infrastructure
The platform is hosted on infrastructure that maintains **SOC 2 Type II** and
**ISO 27001** certifications. These cover the security, availability, and
confidentiality controls of the hosting environment. Our own application-level
controls — tenant isolation, access rules, and an audit trail — sit on top of that
foundation.

### Audit trail
Key actions are recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited or deleted, so there
is a reliable record of who did what and when.

### You stay in control
- Access can be granted and revoked at any time.
- Your data can be exported or deleted on request.
- We can walk your security or IT team through any of the above in detail.
